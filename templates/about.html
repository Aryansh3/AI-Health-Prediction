About the Project

AI Health Risk Prediction System — SDG 3

🎯 Project Objective

The AI Health Risk Prediction System is a beginner-friendly machine learning project that uses basic health information to estimate a person's risk of developing common health conditions such as cardiovascular disease or diabetes.

The primary goals are:

  • Raise health awareness by giving people insight into their personal risk factors.
  • Demonstrate how machine learning can be applied to real-world healthcare data.
  • Support preventive healthcare by encouraging users to adopt healthier lifestyles.
  • Align with UN SDG 3 — Good Health and Well-Being.

📂 Dataset

TypeSynthetic dataset based on UCI Heart Disease / Pima Diabetes patterns
Records1,500 rows
FeaturesAge, Gender, BMI, Blood Pressure, Glucose, Cholesterol, Heart Rate, Smoking, Activity, Family History
TargetBinary — 0 (Low Risk) / 1 (High Risk)
Train/Test Split80% training / 20% testing

🤖 Machine Learning Approach

Three classification models were trained and compared:

Logistic Regression

Estimates the probability that a person is at risk by finding the best linear boundary between at-risk and not-at-risk groups. Simple, fast, and easy to interpret.

Decision Tree

Splits data by asking yes/no questions (e.g., "Is glucose > 126?"). Easy to visualise and explain.

Random Forest

Builds many decision trees and takes a vote. Generally more accurate and robust to noisy data.

Selection: The model with the highest F1-Score on the test set is automatically saved as the active model.

🧠 How the Model Makes a Prediction

1
Training Phase — The model was shown 1,200 historical health records where the outcome (at risk / not at risk) was already known.
2
Pattern Learning — It learned that certain combinations of values (e.g., high blood pressure + high glucose + smoking) are associated with higher risk.
3
New Prediction — When a new user submits their data, the model compares it to those learned patterns and outputs a probability score (0–1).
4
Risk Classification — Probability < 35% → Low Risk; 35–65% → Medium Risk; > 65% → High Risk.

🌍 Connection to SDG 3

UN Sustainable Development Goal 3 targets: ensuring healthy lives and promoting well-being for all at all ages, reducing premature mortality from non-communicable diseases (NCDs) like cardiovascular disease and diabetes, and achieving universal health coverage.

This project supports SDG 3 by:

  • Helping individuals understand their personal health risk factors.
  • Encouraging preventive action before disease develops.
  • Demonstrating how technology can democratise health awareness.
